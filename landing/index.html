What it does

A trade is four moves.
This tracks all four.

A spreadsheet will hold the numbers. It will not tell you whether the loop was worth doing once the interest is counted.

Every loop, start to finish

Each stage dated and priced as it happens, with the position moving from open, to holding, to sold, to closed. Nothing half recorded.

The return after what it cost

Net gain counts the interest you actually paid, not just the price move, and annualizes it so a three day trade and a three month one compare.

Dollars, whatever you borrow

Euro amounts convert at the European Central Bank rate for each transaction's own date, so the euro's own movement lands in the result rather than vanishing.
